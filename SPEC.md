# Medicare Quality Measure Engine — Project Spec

## What this project is

A SQL engine that computes two medication-adherence quality measures on synthetic
member and pharmacy data, plus a Tableau dashboard on the output.

Measures implemented:

1. **Part D Medication Adherence for Diabetes Medications** (PQA / CMS Star Ratings,
   triple-weighted) — PDC >= 0.80
2. **SPD — Statin Therapy for Patients With Diabetes** (NCQA HEDIS), both rates:
   - Rate 1: Received Statin Therapy
   - Rate 2: Statin Adherence 80%

Audience: hiring managers at healthcare-analytics firms. Optimize for correctness
and readability over cleverness.

## Measure logic

Measurement year: **2025** (2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31). Prior year: 2024.

### Shared: PDC calculation

This is the core of the project. Get it right.

1. Order a member's fills within a drug class by `fill_date`.
2. Walk the fills forward maintaining a running "covered through" date.
   - If `fill_date` > covered_through, coverage resumes at `fill_date`.
   - If `fill_date` <= covered_through (early refill), the new supply is **shifted
     forward** to begin the day after covered_through. Surplus days push out; they are
     never double-counted inside an already-covered window.
3. covered_through advances by `days_supply` each fill.
4. Truncate total coverage at the end of the treatment period.
5. `PDC = covered_days / days_in_treatment_period`.
6. Member is adherent if `PDC >= 0.80`.

Implement with window functions (`LAG`, running aggregates) or a recursive CTE.
Do not implement in Python. The SQL is the deliverable.

### Measure 1 — Part D Diabetes Medication Adherence

- **Denominator:** members with at least **two** fills of a DIABETES-class drug on
  different dates during the measurement year.
- **Treatment period:** first diabetes-med fill date (IPSD) through the earlier of
  year end, `death_date`, or `coverage_end`.
- **Numerator:** members in the denominator with PDC >= 0.80 on DIABETES-class drugs.
- **Exclusions:** hospice at any point in the measurement year. (Real spec also
  excludes ESRD and certain insulin scenarios — NOT implemented, document this.)

### Measure 2 — SPD (HEDIS)

Two nested rates. Rate 2's denominator IS Rate 1's numerator.

- **Age:** 40–75 as of **2025-12-31**. Compute against the fixed anchor date, never
  against the current system date.
- **Continuous enrollment:** enrolled for the measurement year and the prior year.
- **Diabetes identification** — either path qualifies:
  - at least two `DIABETES` condition rows on **different dates** in 2024 or 2025, OR
  - at least one DIABETES-class drug fill in 2024 or 2025
- **Exclusions:** any `ASCVD` condition row on or before 2025-12-31; hospice.
  (Real spec also excludes ESRD, cirrhosis, myalgia/myopathy/rhabdomyolysis,
  pregnancy, IVF, palliative care, advanced illness with frailty — NOT implemented,
  document this.)
- **Rate 1 numerator:** at least one STATIN-class fill during the measurement year.
- **Rate 2 treatment period:** earliest statin fill date in 2025 (IPSD) through
  2025-12-31. Note this differs from Measure 1's treatment period end handling.
- **Rate 2 numerator:** PDC >= 0.80 on STATIN-class drugs over that treatment period.

## Required edge cases in the generator

Hardcode these members with known IDs so results can be hand-verified. Generate ~500
members total; these must be among them.

| member_id | scenario | expected |
|---|---|---|
| M_PERFECT | clean 30-day fills all year, no gaps | PDC ~1.00 |
| M_EARLY | refills every 20 days with 30-day supply | tests the shift; PDC ~1.00, NOT >1.00 |
| M_GAP60 | 60-day gap mid-year | PDC ~0.83 |
| M_ONEFILL | exactly one diabetes fill | excluded from Measure 1 denominator |
| M_DIED | dies 2025-08-15 | treatment period truncated at death |
| M_LATEENROLL | coverage starts 2025-03-01 | fails SPD continuous enrollment |
| M_ASCVD | diabetes + ASCVD dx | excluded from SPD |
| M_SWITCH | atorvastatin then rosuvastatin mid-year | continuous coverage across drugs |
| M_HOSPICE | hospice dx in 2025 | excluded from both measures |

**A PDC above 1.00 anywhere in the output is a bug.** Add an assertion for this.

## README requirements

Keep it under 400 words. Required sections, in order:

1. **What this is** — one paragraph. Must state in the first two sentences that the
   data is synthetic and generated by this repo.
2. **Why these measures matter** — Part D adherence measures are triple-weighted in
   CMS Star Ratings; SPD is an NCQA HEDIS measure. Two sentences.
3. **The interesting problem** — the early-refill shift, in three sentences.
4. **Results** — the computed rates. Only real numbers from a real run.
5. **Verification** — that edge cases were hand-checked against spreadsheet arithmetic.
6. **Not implemented** — the full honest list of skipped exclusions and simplifications.
7. **Links** — Tableau Public dashboard.

No badges. No emoji. No "🚀 Features" section. No claims about accuracy or performance
that were not measured.
